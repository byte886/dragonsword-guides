Johnny — Venom DPS

The leader of Johnny Mercenaries, Johnny is a fast melee Venom DPS who closes distance quickly and keeps Poison pressure on enemies. He excels as the ground continuation after aerial teammates finish their pressure.

⚡ Quick Answer

Johnny is an A-tier Venom DPS. Equip Karma "Wandering Blade", 3-piece Ruinsbreaker + 2-piece Venomfog Shade, and prioritize ATK, Crit DMG, and Crit Chance with Resolve runes. His best team is Charlotte + Reina (or Eileen) for a Poison/Airborne composition. Johnny maintains Poison while Charlotte detonates it for massive burst.

🎯 Playstyle

Johnny leads from the front with fast dual-blade combos that close distance and apply Poison. He is the game's premier Venom applier — quick, mobile, and relentless. His role in a team is to build and maintain Poison stacks on the ground, then let Charlotte detonate the Poison with her burst skills. He returns to the floor quickly during his combos, making him the ideal follow-up after Charlotte's aerial pressure ends.

💡 Combat Tips

Maintain Poison

Your primary job is keeping Poison on the target. Don't let stacks fall off.

Ground Continuation

After Charlotte's aerial combo ends, swap to Johnny to continue pressure on the ground.

Swap for Burst

When Poison is fully stacked, swap to Charlotte for the detonation burst.

Crit DMG Priority

Invest heavily in Crit DMG — Johnny's multi-hit skills benefit enormously from high crit damage.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Trying to make Johnny a second Charlotte — he is a ground fighter, not an aerial combo character
  • Swapping to Charlotte before Poison is fully stacked — the detonation needs max Poison
  • Building defensive stats — Johnny is a glass cannon; invest in damage
