Tarte — Healer

One of only two dedicated healers in the game (alongside Lute). Tarte provides healing, crowd-control removal, and can inflict Shock and Stun to support combo teams.

Orbis Kingdom
Staff / Healing Magic

⚡ Quick Answer

Tarte is a B-tier healer/support. Her optimal build is still being tested by the community, but she excels at keeping the team alive and removing CC effects. Her best team is Ornette + Sion for the Shock/Thunderbolt composition. She can also slot into any team that needs healing as a flex support alongside Lute.

📝 Note

Tarte's optimal Karma, gear, and rune builds are still being tested by the community as of August 2026. Recommendations on this page are provisional and will be updated as the meta settles.

🎯 Playstyle

Tarte is a dedicated healer who keeps her team alive through sustained healing and crowd-control removal. Unlike Lute (who also provides armor break and buffs), Tarte focuses more on raw healing and Shock/Stun utility. She can inflict Shock during combat, which enables Ornette's Thunderbolt detonations. Her lack of damage limits her to support duty, but in tough content where survival matters, she is invaluable.

💡 Combat Tips

Cleanse Proactively

Use Cleanse when allies are CC'd — don't wait for them to take damage.

Heal Between Swaps

Tarte's healing persists on swapped-out characters, so top up before swapping to DPS.

Apply Shock for Ornette

Your Shock attacks enable Ornette's Thunderbolt detonation — don't neglect offense entirely.

Save Divine Heal

Use the massive party heal for emergency situations or boss ultimate attacks.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Treating Tarte as a DPS — her damage is low; focus on healing and Shock utility
  • Forgetting to Cleanse — CC removal is one of her most valuable abilities
  • Using Divine Heal when the party is already at full HP — save it for emergencies
