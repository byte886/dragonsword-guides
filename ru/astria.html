Astria — Ranged Stun + Break DPS

A ranged Breaker who rains magic bolts from a distance to apply Stun and shatter enemy armor. Astria trades mobility for massive ranged damage, making her ideal when teammates keep enemies occupied.

Orbis Kingdom

⚡ Quick Answer

Astria is an A-tier ranged Stun/Break specialist. Equip Karma "Windflowers Bloom Through the Wind", 3-piece Ruinsbreaker + 2-piece Holy Arbiter, and prioritize ATK, Crit Chance, and Crit DMG. Her best teams are Kalien + Roxy (Stun/Bleed) or Castella + Lute (double Break). Stack Crit Rate — her bolts hit hard when they crit.

🎯 Playstyle

Astria fires volleys of magic bolts from range that apply Stun and Break enemy armor. She is less mobile than other heroes but compensates with high ranged damage and crowd control. She shines when paired with teammates who keep enemies in place — while they occupy the target, Astria rains bolts to break armor and stun, setting up Signal Skill combos for the team.

💡 Combat Tips

Keep Distance

Astria is a ranged hero — position her at max range to avoid damage while breaking armor.

Stack Crit Rate

Her multi-hit volleys benefit enormously from high Crit Rate. Aim for 80%+.

Break First, Then Stun

Focus on breaking armor with heavy shots, then use Stun Rain to lock enemies down.

Pair with Occupiers

Teammates like Castella or Kalien keep enemies busy while Astria fires from safety.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Playing Astria in melee — she is fragile and needs distance
  • Ignoring Crit Rate in favor of pure ATK — her kit is designed around crits
  • Using Break Bolt before armor is broken — save it for the Stun window
