Roxy — The Crimson Archer

Leader of the Red Fox Mercenaries, Roxy wields a bow to stack Bleed from a safe distance. The most approachable Bleed character in the roster — Crimson Spiral dashes in applying Bleed and Stun, while Bloody Shower rains arrows that bleed every enemy in a line.

⚡ Quick Answer

Roxy is an A-tier ranged Bleed DPS and the easiest Bleed character to pick up. Equip her Exclusive Karma "A noble Lady's Wild Hobby", 3-piece Ruinsbreaker + 2-piece Bloodstained Chase, and prioritize Crit Chance, ATK Power, and Crit DMG. Her best team is Theresia + Roxy + Lute — the strongest Bleed/Stun composition in the current meta.

🎯 Playstyle

Roxy is the most approachable Bleed character in the roster. Almost every skill applies Bleed, making her consistent against high-health enemies and bosses where sustained damage-over-time adds up. She fights at range with her bow, using Crimson Spiral as a gap-closer that applies both Bleed and Stun, then follows up with Kick Storm on stunned targets.

She is not the highest Bleed ceiling — Theresia holds that title — but her ease of use and ranged safety make her reliable across all content. She shines in long boss encounters where Bleed ticks repeatedly.

💡 Combat Tips

Open with Crimson Spiral

Dash in applying Bleed + Stun, then immediately follow with Kick Storm on the stunned target for maximum damage.

Bloody Shower for Groups

Fires in a straight line and Bleeds every enemy it touches. Great for clearing trash mobs.

Stack Bleed to 2+

Blood Blade Strike applies Suppression at 2+ Bleed stacks — a stronger control than basic Bleed.

Mount Giant Bosses

At 2+ Bleed stacks, chain attacks while riding the boss for significant damage.

Prioritize Awakening 5

+15% damage to Bleeding enemies and +15% DEF Ignore is her biggest power spike.

Stay at Range

Bow attacks keep Roxy safe. Use Slide Shot to reposition when enemies get close.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Using Kick Storm without Stun — it requires a stunned target; without Theresia or another Stun source, this skill is unavailable
  • Building pure ATK without Crit — Awakening 3 and 5 reward Crit; aim for ~80% Crit Rate
  • Playing her like burst DPS — Roxy is sustained damage through Bleed; long fights are where she shines
  • Ignoring Air Dodge — the bounce provides damage and repositioning; Awakening 3 adds Crit DMG stacks per hit
