Lute — The Protagonist

The main character and one of only two healers in the game. A versatile utility hero who fits into almost any team as the third slot, providing healing, armor break and team buffs.

Starting Hero
Amplitude Trait
Stun / Recovery / Knockdown
Melee Greatsword
⚡ Quick Answer

Lute is the best beginner-friendly hero and the safest investment in the game. Free unlock, heals the party, breaks armor, and works in literally any team. Build him defensively, awaken to 6★ for party healing, and use him as your third slot whenever a fight feels too difficult.

⚔️ Best Build

Defensive / Support Build (Recommended)

Gear Sets

2 Holy Arbiter + 3 Ruinsbreaker

Holy Arbiter boosts healing output, Ruinsbreaker provides DEF Ignore to maintain personal damage contribution.

Main Stats

ATK Power Up → Critical DMG → Critical Hit Chance

For a pure support build: DEF, HP, Healing Effectiveness

Runes

Resolve (primary) / Amplification (alternative)

Exclusive Karma

A Sword Summoned for a Friend — Obtained from "Throbbing Heart" drops from daily bosses (refreshes at 9 AM).

Reina + Castella + Lute Best Airborne team Extends Airborne duration, healing sustain
Ornette + Castella + Lute Heavy armor bosses Replaces Alex, provides healing and survival
Sion + Ornette + Lute Shock / Thunderbolt team Support healing and ATK buff
Any duo + Lute General purpose Add when your team lacks stability

🪽 Airborne Combo Loop

Reina + Castella + Lute

  1. Reina uses Rising Cutter or Wind Slayer to launch the target
  2. As the target rises, execute Reina's Tempest Signal Skill
  3. Switch to Lute — use his Signal Skill to extend Airborne duration
  4. Cut to Castella — slams the target to the ground for massive damage
  5. Repeat the loop to maintain Airborne window control

✅ Strengths

🎁

Free Starting Hero

Available from the very beginning. No tickets, no unlock grind — just play and invest.

💚

One of Two Healers

Only Lute and Tarte can heal. At 6★ awakening, Lute provides party-wide healing.

🔄

Universal Flex Slot

Fits into virtually any team as the third slot. The definition of a utility pick.

🛡️

Dual Ailment Support

Supports both Stun and Knockdown team archetypes. Also breaks boss armor.

📈

Team Buffs

Awakening nodes provide team ATK boost and party healing. Makes everyone better.

🏆

Survival Insurance

In long boss fights, Lute keeps the party alive when pure damage teams would wipe.

❌ Weaknesses

  • Lower personal damage — As a support, his DPS doesn't compare to pure damage dealers
  • Not in absolute best endgame team — The top-end team (Theresia + Reina + Castella) doesn't run Lute
  • Requires awakening — Party healing only unlocks at 6★; early-game healing is limited
  • Defensive playstyle — Built for survival, not for carrying damage. Not a main DPS.

🎮 Combat Tips

  1. Emergency switch — When party HP is low, switch to Lute and pop Circle of Healing
  2. Armor break opener — Start boss fights with Lute to break power armor, then switch to DPS
  3. Airborne extender — In Airborne teams, switch to Lute after Reina launches to extend the window
  4. Tank hits — In emergencies, switch to Lute (defensive build) to absorb damage
  5. Team buff uptime — Keep Lute's awakening buffs active for consistent party ATK boost
  6. Flex slot mindset — Adjust between damage and survival based on the fight's demands

⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌

Building as Main DPS

Lute is a support. Investing in pure damage wastes his healing and utility potential.

❌

Ignoring Awakening

6★ party healing is his defining feature. Don't skip awakening priority.

❌

Running in Every Team

Pure damage endgame teams may not need Lute. Know when to swap him out.

❌

Skipping DEF Stats

Full defensive build lets Lute actually tank hits. Glass cannon Lute dies instantly.
