Raids & Runes Guide

Raids are the primary source of runes — powerful stat-boosting items socketed into 4-star+ gear. Unlock rune slots with Crafting Tools, farm Rune Dungeons for SSR runes, and tackle multiplayer raids with up to 3 players.

📖 Reading time: ~7 min 💎 5 Rune Types 👥 3-Player Co-op
⚡ Quick Answer

Runes are stat boosts attached to 4-star+ gear. Best DPS priority: Resolve (ATK) > Amplification (Crit DMG) > Concentration (Crit Rate). Unlock rune slots with Anonymous Artisan's Crafting Tools (from raids). Farm Rune Dungeon at Lv62+ for guaranteed SSR runes. Raids support up to 3 players via lobby + invite code. Target: 80% Crit Rate + 150% Crit Damage.

💎 Rune System Overview

  • Runes are stat-boosting items attached to equipment
  • Each rune targets one specific stat
  • Primarily obtained through raids
  • 5 rarity tiers: Damaged → Intact → Refined → Radiant → Perfect
  • RNG independently affects rune power — high rarity doesn't always mean a big upgrade

🔮 All 5 Rune Types

Rune Stat Boost Best For
Resolve Flat ATK (Attack Power) All DPS heroes. Universal best-in-slot.
Amplification Critical Damage DPS with high crit rate. Raid performance spikes at 150%+.
Concentration Critical Hit Chance DPS struggling to hit crit rate cap. Usually better via gear/Karma.
Guardian Flat DEF (Defense) Tanks / supports in harder raid content.
Vitality Max HP Survival in difficult raids. Doesn't boost damage.

🎯 Best Rune Priority

DPS Heroes

  1. Resolve — Flat ATK applies to all damage calculations. Universally useful.
  2. Amplification — Crit Damage. Raid performance noticeably improves at 150%+.
  3. Concentration — Crit Rate. Consider only if your rate is unstable; usually more efficient via gear and Karma.

Tank / Support Heroes

  1. Guardian — Flat DEF for survivability
  2. Vitality — Max HP for buffer against burst damage
💡 Target Stats

Aim for approximately 80% Crit Rate and 150% Crit Damage on your main DPS. Once you hit these benchmarks, shift focus to ATK and DEF Ignore.

🔓 How to Unlock Rune Slots

Rune slots are not pre-unlocked. They must be opened manually:

  1. Select gear that is 4-star or higher
  2. Click the locked rune slot on the gear, or press Triangle/Y while inspecting
  3. Spend one Anonymous Artisan's Crafting Tool to open the slot
  4. Each slot requires its own Crafting Tool
  5. Insert the desired rune — the total stat boost shows in the gear summary
⚠️ Common Pitfall

Running out of Anonymous Artisan's Crafting Tools is the #1 reason players get stuck on rune progression. Only unlock slots on your main team's permanent gear — don't waste tools on temporary equipment.

⛏️ Where to Farm Runes

1. Rune Dungeon (魔源旋涡)

  • Unlocks at Lv50
  • Before Lv50, you can challenge 5-10 levels above your current level
  • Difficulty spikes at Lv55 — need equal-level gear to clear
  • After Lv62, each clear guarantees 1 SSR rune
  • Long-term best-in-slot source for free-to-play players

2. Raids (鏖战副本)

  • Unlocks at Lv50 (same time as Rune Dungeon)
  • Low tiers drop low-rank runes
  • High-tier bosses drop high-quality Gold / SSR runes
  • Also drop Anonymous Artisan's Crafting Tools
  • Serves as supplementary material source alongside Rune Dungeon

👥 Multiplayer Raid Mechanics

  • Every co-op session requires creating a lobby and sending an invite code to friends
  • Only the lobby host can select dungeon/raid/hunt and specific difficulty
  • If settings are wrong, you must disband the entire lobby and recreate
  • No immediate retry option — after completion you're kicked out and must recreate the lobby
  • No open-world co-op — only dungeons, raids and hunts support multiplayer
  • Raids allow inviting up to 2 other players (3 total)

Matchmaking System

  • After selecting mode and boss, the game checks for existing lobbies with the same content
  • If none found, it creates a new lobby that other players can join
  • Can set rooms to Private — only invited players can join

🎮 Game Mode Comparison

Mode Players Content Rewards
Dungeons Solo / Co-op Trait dungeons, currency dungeons Gear, materials, gold
Raids Up to 3 players High-difficulty boss fights Runes, high-tier materials, Crafting Tools
Hunts Solo / Co-op World boss / elite hunts Upgrade materials, Karma materials

📜 Raid Core Strategy

1

Rune Priority

DPS: Resolve > Amplification > Concentration. Tank/Support: Guardian > Vitality. Target 80% crit / 150% crit dmg.

2

Slot Management

Only unlock slots on 4-star+ gear. Prioritize your main team's core gear. Don't waste Crafting Tools on temp gear.

3

Co-op Communication

Coordinate team composition with friends beforehand. Host selects difficulty. Use invite codes if matchmaking fails.

4

Difficulty Progression

Start low at Lv50. Lv55 needs equal-level gear. Lv62+ is the long-term target for guaranteed SSR runes.
