Johnny — 剧毒DPS

乔尼佣兵团的领袖，Johnny是一名快速近战剧毒DPS，能迅速接近敌人并保持中毒压力。他擅长在空中队友结束压制后作为地面接续输出。

乔尼佣兵团
双刀
中毒/浮空
剧毒DPS/地面接续
⚡ 快速答案

Johnny是A级剧毒DPS。装备专属Karma"Wandering Blade"、3件Ruinsbreaker + 2件Venomfog Shade，优先攻击力、暴击伤害和暴击率，符文用决意。最佳配队是Charlotte + Reina（或Eileen）组成剧毒/浮空队。Johnny维持中毒，Charlotte引爆造成爆发。

🎯 玩法定位

Johnny以快速双刀连击冲锋在前，接近敌人并施加中毒。他是游戏中最主要的剧毒施加者——敏捷、机动、 relentless。他在队伍中的角色是在地面建立并维持中毒层数，然后让Charlotte用爆发技能引爆中毒。他在连击过程中能快速回到地面，因此是Charlotte空中压制结束后的理想接续。

💡 战斗技巧

维持中毒

Your primary job is keeping Poison on the target. Don't let stacks fall off.

地面接续

After Charlotte's aerial combo ends, swap to Johnny to continue pressure on the ground.

换人爆发

When Poison is fully stacked, swap to Charlotte for the detonation burst.

暴击伤害优先

Invest heavily in Crit DMG — Johnny's multi-hit skills benefit enormously from high crit damage.

⚠️ 常见误区

  • Trying to make Johnny a second Charlotte — he is a ground fighter, not an aerial combo character
  • Swapping to Charlotte before Poison is fully stacked — the detonation needs max Poison
  • Building defensive stats — Johnny is a glass cannon; invest in damage
