Lentus Rebellion · S Tier

Charlotte — The Informant

A high-risk, high-reward melee assassin DPS. Applies Poison then converts to Airborne to open combo chains. Stealth clones add extra pressure. Excels with skilled play, punishes mistakes hard.

Twin Daggers
Venom Trait
Poison / Airborne / Knockdown
Exchange Shop
⚡ Quick Answer

Charlotte is one of the most versatile S-tier heroes. She applies Poison then converts to Airborne, enabling continuous Signal Skill and Tag Combo chains. Best team: Charlotte + Johnny + Reina. Build with 3 Ruinsbreaker + 2 Venomfog Shade. High skill ceiling — not for players who want safe ranged play.

Exclusive Karma Effect

When the clone re-casts Q skill, it leaps in the facing direction and explodes — adding significant extra damage to your rotation.

Stat Priority Order

  1. Attack Power — Charlotte's job is to make every opportunity deal damage
  2. Status Ailment Efficiency — Poison, Knockdown and Airborne pressure convert solo pressure into Signal Skill sequences
  3. Crit Rate & Crit Damage — Makes assassination windows scale properly
  4. Survival — Only add if deaths are actually preventing clears. Too defensive reduces Charlotte's value

🔄 Combat Flow (6 Steps)

  1. Test launch response — At the start of combat, test how the enemy reacts to Airborne
  2. Keep the camera readable — Melee characters suffer from bad camera angles. Position carefully
  3. Use lit teammate inputs — When Signal Skill windows appear, switch promptly
  4. Use Reina for space — When you need to disengage, switch to Reina to reposition safely
  5. Return to Charlotte — Reset combat rhythm through teammate switches
  6. Poison first, then Airborne — Stack Poison first, then convert to Airborne to open combos

✅ Strengths

🏆

S-Tier Versatility

One of the easiest heroes to build around. Fits into more team compositions than almost any other hero.

☠️

Dual Ailment Power

Applies both Poison AND Airborne. Converts Poison pressure into Airborne combo openings.

👤

Stealth Clone

Invisible skill creates a clone. With Exclusive Karma, clone leaps and explodes for extra damage.

💨

High Mobility

Assassin定位 with fast approach and disengage. Quickly converts between ground and aerial combos.

🔗

Combo Driver

Opens team Signal Skill and Tag Combo sequences. The engine that makes aggressive teams work.

📈

Early Game Strong

Poison performs exceptionally well in early game (before level 50). Great for progression.

❌ Weaknesses

  • High skill requirement — High investment, high return. Requires precise dodging and timing
  • Melee punishment — Less forgiving than ranged configurations. Mistakes are heavily punished
  • Camera issues — Melee combat can lead to unreadable camera angles
  • Poison falls off late — After level 50, bosses may have increased Poison resistance
  • Needs teammates — Best performance requires Reina or similar support to shine

🎮 Combat Tips

  1. Test Airborne first — Always test enemy Airborne response at the start of combat
  2. Keep camera readable — Watch your positioning in melee to avoid camera obstruction
  3. Use lit teammate inputs — Switch immediately when Signal Skill windows appear
  4. Reina for spacing — Switch to Reina when you need to create space or reposition
  5. Poison opener — Stack Poison first, then convert to Airborne to open combos
  6. Clone explosion — Use Exclusive Karma effect to make the clone leap and explode for extra damage
  7. Fast reset — Reset combat rhythm through teammate switches when pressure drops

⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌

Too Defensive

Over-investing in survival stats reduces Charlotte's damage value. Only add defense if deaths are real problems.

❌

Ignoring Ailment Efficiency

Without Status Ailment Efficiency, Poison stacks too slowly. This stat is core to her rotation.

❌

Wasting Clone

Not leveraging the stealth clone and Exclusive Karma explosion wastes a significant portion of her damage.

❌

Chasing Bad Camera

Pursuing pressure when the camera is unreadable gets you punished. Disengage and reposition.

❌

No Reina Support

Charlotte's best performance needs teammates like Reina. Running her solo-carry limits her ceiling.

❌

Playing Like Ranged

Charlotte is a melee assassin. If you want safe ranged play, pick Cerese or Othello instead.
