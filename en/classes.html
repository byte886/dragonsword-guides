Hero Classification

Classes & Combat Roles

DragonSword: Awakening does NOT have a traditional class system (no warrior/mage/priest selection or job advancement). Instead, there are 19 fixed heroes, each with a unique Hero Trait, status effect specialty and combat role. Here's how they're classified.

📖 Reading time: ~6 min 🦸 19 Heroes ⚔️ 5 Factions
💡 Key Point

No traditional classes. Unlike most RPGs where you pick a class and can job-change, DragonSword uses 19 unique fixed heroes. Each has its own Hero Trait, weapon, status effect specialty and combat role. You can't change a hero's "class" — you build teams by combining heroes with complementary roles and status effects.

⚔️ vs Traditional Class Systems

Traditional Class System DragonSword: Awakening
Pick one class, can job-advance 19 fixed heroes, no advancement
Same-class characters play similarly Every hero has unique Trait and skills
Class determines gear and skills Hero determines status effects and role
Tank/DPS/Healer trinity Flexible — heroes can fill multiple roles
Class balance patches affect many Balance patches target individual heroes

🧩 How to Build Teams by Role

1

Pick Your Main DPS

Start with 1-2 strong DPS heroes (Theresia, Reina, Charlotte, Lute). This is your damage core.

2

Add a Connector

Roxy or Johnny extends combos and bridges between heroes, boosting overall team DPS.

3

Match Status Effects

Build around a status type: Bleed team (Roxy+Theresia+Lute), Poison team (Charlotte+Johnny), Air team (Reina+Castella).

4

Add Utility/Support

Dana for shields, Ornette for shock control, Castella for airborne. Flex based on content.
