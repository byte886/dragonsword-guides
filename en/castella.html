Castella — The Wild Axe

An eccentric elf fascinated by archaeology, Castella wields a giant two-handed battle axe to shatter enemy armor and control the battlefield. Unlocked for free in Chapter 1, she remains a top-tier pick for breaking super armor and setting up Signal Skill combos.

⚡ Quick Answer

Castella is an A-tier Breaker who excels at shattering super armor and controlling crowds with Stun and Airborne. Equip her Exclusive Karma "Noisy Duet", 3-piece Ruinsbreaker + 2-piece Azure Gale, and prioritize ATK Power, Crit DMG, and Crit Chance. Her best team is Castella + Astria + Lute for armor break, or Reina + Castella + Lute for airborne juggle combos.

🎯 Playstyle

Castella is one of the first characters you unlock and remains effective deep into endgame. Her kit is straightforward but powerful: break enemy super armor with heavy axe hits, then punish with Stun Strike or launch groups into the air with Rising Heavy Strike.

Whirlwind gathers enemies together while dealing AoE damage, and Wild Roar pushes enemies back while reducing their DEF (after Awakening 3). She has no complex mechanics — simple, dependable crowd control that makes her a fixture in most team compositions.

💡 Combat Tips

Break Armor First

Stun Strike only works on enemies with broken super armor. Focus heavy hits to break their guard before using it.

Whirlwind to Group

Use Whirlwind to gather scattered enemies, then follow with Stun Strike or Rising Heavy Strike on the group.

Awakening 1 is Key

Adds Knockdown to Stun Strike, giving you two control effects in one skill — a massive power spike.

Roar Reduces DEF

After Awakening 3, Wild Roar reduces enemy DEF, significantly increasing the whole team's damage.

Free Early Investment

Castella unlocks automatically in Chapter 1. Investing in her early pays off throughout the entire game.

Move During Whirlwind

You can move slowly while spinning — use this to reposition and catch more enemies in the AoE.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Using Stun Strike on unbroken armor — it requires broken super armor; break first, then stun
  • Using Rising Heavy Strike without Knockdown — it requires a knocked-down target to activate
  • Building pure DPS — Castella's value is control and armor breaking; position her as a control character
  • Ignoring Whirlwind mobility — you can move during Whirlwind to chase enemies or reposition
