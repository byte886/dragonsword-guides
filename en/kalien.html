Kalien — The Spirit Mage

A spirit mage fighting alongside the fire fox spirit Sur. Kalien excels at stacking Burn through fox clones and delivering burst damage with Stun via Fox Grab and Finale. The first hero individually revealed for DragonSword: Awakening.

Red Fox Mercenary Crew
Blazeflame Trait
Burn / Stun
Fox Spirit Sur
⚡ Quick Answer

Kalien is an A-tier Burn DPS who controls the battlefield through her fox spirit Sur and clone army. She stacks Burn rapidly across wide areas, then cashes out with Fox Grab and Finale for heavy burst plus Stun. Best paired with Kalsion and Aria for a top-tier Burn/Airborne team. Build her with 3-piece Blazing Flame + 2-piece Ruinsbreaker, prioritizing ATK, Crit Chance and Crit DMG.

🔥 Combat Style

Kalien's kit centers on controlling targets with Stun while Sur's fire damage chains underneath. Her role in the three-step combo system is to stack Stun on a target through Active Skills, then either finish with her own Signal Skill or switch out so an ally can chain a Signal Skill off the primed Stun window.

Sur is a fox spirit imbued with the power of fire. Kalien can summon fox clones that autonomously attack and apply Burn across a wide area, making her exceptional at clearing trash mobs and maintaining pressure on elite groups.

👥 Best Team Compositions

Theresia + Astria + Kalien Bleed / Stun hybrid Adds Burn pressure and AoE clear alongside Theresia's Bleed

Aria + Kalien + Kalsion — Burn/Airborne Loop

  1. Aria applies both Burn AND Airborne simultaneously — the best enabler for this comp
  2. Kalien deploys fox clones to rapidly stack Burn across the target area
  3. Kalien uses Fox Grab to burst the burned target and apply Stun
  4. Kalsion finishes with Weapon Blaze-enhanced damage under Burn + Airborne
  5. Kalien's fox provides autonomous damage and Stun between switches

✅ Strengths

🔥

Excellent Burn Pressure

Fox clones apply Burn across wide areas, making it easy to maintain DoT on groups.

💥

AoE Clear

Incredible at clearing trash mobs and elite groups with flashy area attacks.

⚡

Stun Utility

Finale inflicts both Burn and Stun — solid crowd control alongside heavy damage.

🦊

Autonomous Damage

Sur and fox clones continue fighting while you switch to other heroes.

❌ Weaknesses

  • Position-dependent — Much of Kalien's damage depends on fox spirit placement and timing
  • Mobile enemies — Fast bosses can escape fox attack areas, reducing burst potential
  • Setup required — Needs Burn enablers (especially Aria) to reach peak damage
  • Not a solo carry — Excels in a coordinated Burn team, less effective alone

🎮 Combat Tips

  1. Deploy clones first — Send out fox clones before committing to Fox Grab so Burn is already stacked
  2. Time Finale with Stun — Use Finale when the target is surrounded by clones for maximum Burn + Stun burst
  3. Switch after Stun — After applying Stun with Finale, switch to Kalsion or Aria to chain Signal Skills
  4. Position for groups — Fox clones shine against grouped enemies; single-target fights reduce her value
  5. Manage Burn stacks — Keep reapplying Burn through clones before it expires for sustained DoT
