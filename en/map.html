Map & World Guide

Explore the Continent of Orbis — a vast open world with 13 Statues of Organa (Dark Souls-style bonfires), hidden underwater dungeons, world bosses and resource gathering nodes. Restore statues to unlock healing and rare loot.

📖 Reading time: ~7 min 🗺️ 15+ Areas 🗿 13 Statues
⚡ Quick Answer

The world is called the Continent of Orbis. 13 Statues of Organa act as bonfires — restore them for healing charges and Silver Keys to open nearby locked chests. Each open-world area has at least one statue. Two hidden dungeons are underwater and don't appear on the world map. Use the community interactive map (dragonswordawakening.th.gl) with 17,833 marked locations.

🗿 Statues of Organa (Bonfire System)

Similar to Dark Souls bonfires. Find scattered fragments and return them to unlock 6 healing charges (regenerate over time).

  • Every open-world area has at least one statue
  • Successfully restoring a statue rewards a Silver Key
  • Silver Keys open locked chests near the statue (contain high-tier loot)
  • Always restore nearby statues before entering high-level areas for healing anchors

Detailed Fragment Locations

Shaded Tree Hill (4 fragments)

  1. Behind a collapsed wall around the statue
  2. North of the statue by the water, hidden in bushes
  3. East of the statue, near an enemy camp
  4. Southeast of the statue, near a cliff

Autumnleaf Zone (3 fragments)

  1. Behind stone railings around the statue base
  2. On a rock in the lake's southeast side. Can't swim directly (fragment sinks) — find a tree by the lake, interact and hold forward to push it as a bridge
  3. On top of a western stone pillar — ride a Familiar to climb up

🔒 Hidden Areas & Dungeons

  • Shadowed Woods — Requires talking to Alvwyn first, then using Aria's fire skill to burn spider webs covering the statue (physical attacks don't work)
  • Two Hidden Dungeons — Both are underwater and don't appear on the world map. Explore underwater areas to find them
  • Vein Crystals farming — Each run can farm ~30 Vein Crystals

💡 Exploration Tips

1

Restore Statues First

Always restore nearby statues before exploring high-level areas. Healing charges save you from long walks back.

2

Use Interactive Maps

The community maps mark 17,000+ locations. Use them to find chests, materials and hidden areas efficiently.

3

Check Underwater

Two hidden dungeons are underwater. Dive in lakes and coastal areas to find secret entrances.

4

Bring Cooking Buffs

Slicing dishes reduce Familiar stamina consumption for flight, swim and climb. Essential for long exploration sessions.
