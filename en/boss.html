Boss Guide

Bosses have heavy power armor that normal attacks can't break. Fill the ailment gauge to stagger them, then trigger Signal Skills in the narrow window. Break armor first, then burst. Dodge always beats finishing a combo.

📖 Reading time: ~6 min 👹 Story + World Bosses 🛡️ Power Armor System
⚡ Quick Answer

Most bosses have power armor or shields — break them first before you can deal real HP damage. Fill the ailment gauge to stagger, then switch heroes in the narrow window for Signal Skill bursts. Best all-around boss team: Theresia + Castella + Lute. For high-armor bosses use Ornette/Alex + Castella. For high-HP bosses use Theresia + Roxy for Bleed. Always dodge instead of finishing a combo.

🛡️ Boss Core Mechanics

  • Elites and Bosses have heavy power armor that normal attacks can't break
  • Attacks fill the ailment gauge — when full, the enemy enters an ailment state (stagger)
  • Ailment states open Signal Skill opportunities for massive damage
  • You must switch characters within the narrow window while the ailment is active
  • Shields regenerate after a short time — maintain continuous pressure

📖 Tutorial Boss: Orc Warrior (Lv.3)

First Boss in the Game

  • Location: Behind a giant gate in a cave
  • Features: Giant orc that summons goblins during combat
  • Ally: Johnny assists you during this fight

Dual-Bar Mechanic (HP + Shield)

  • Must continuously attack to break the shield before you can effectively damage HP
  • After shield breaks, defense drops and you can defeat efficiently
  • Shield recharges after a short time — don't let up

Combat Tips

  1. Press Shift to dodge roll away from boss attacks
  2. Move forward and attack after dodging
  3. Johnny's attacks can juggle (launch) the boss
  4. Jump to attack the airborne Orc Warrior
  5. Use Tag Combo attacks to finish faster

🎯 Universal Boss Strategy

1. Break Armor First

  • Most bosses have shields or power armor
  • Break the shield before you can deal effective damage
  • Shields regenerate — maintain continuous pressure
  • Armor-breaker heroes: Alex, Ornette, Lute

2. Ailment Chain Combos

  • Use Active Skills to apply status ailments
  • Wait for teammate portraits to light up
  • Switch within the narrow window to trigger Signal Skills
  • Repeat the cycle instead of sticking to one hero

3. Dodge > Combo

  • Never take damage just to finish a combo
  • Boss attacks have clear wind-up animations — dodge in time
  • Reorganize your offense after dodging

4. Use Airborne / Knockdown

  • Airborne enemies can't counter-attack
  • Knocked-down enemies take more damage
  • Note: Super Armor prevents these states — bring break options

📋 Known Boss Categories

📖

Story Bosses

Orc Warrior (tutorial), Dragon (prologue cinematic), and others encountered during main quest progression.

🌍

World Bosses

Scattered across open world areas. Daily respawn. Provide upgrade materials and Karma cards. Check interactive map for locations.

🗿

Statue Bosses

Triggered during Statue of Organa restoration: Ruined Temple (Lv60), Barren Trading Post (Lv60 Troll), Seagull Village (Lv25 Beast), Echo Canyon.

🏰

Raid Bosses

Multiplayer raid bosses. Drop runes and high-tier materials. Require coordinated team play.

🍳 Before You Fight: Preparation

  • Eat a Boiling buff stew (15-min ATK/Crit buffs) before challenging bosses
  • Bring at least one Revival Tea in case a teammate falls
  • Equip 3-pc Ruinsbreaker on your main DPS for DEF Ignore
  • Restore the nearest Statue of Organa for a nearby healing anchor
  • Match your team to the boss type (armor vs HP vs general)
