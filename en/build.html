Hero Builds

Complete Build Guide

Best gear sets, Karma weapons, main stats and runes for every hero. Build assumptions: fully awakened heroes, not max-level Karma or perfect substats.

📖 Reading time: ~10 min ⚔️ 16 Heroes Built 🔄 Updated Aug 2026
⚡ Quick Build Formula
  1. Fully awaken your hero first (unlocks passives and enhanced set effects)
  2. Use 3-piece Ruinsbreaker as core (DEF Ignore for almost everyone)
  3. Target ~80% Crit Rate and ~150% Crit Damage
  4. Add 2-piece element set matching your hero's ailment
  5. Team: one hero applies ailment, next detonates it

📊 Universal Stat Priorities

🎯

Crit Rate ~80%

Your first target. Below 80% you lose too much damage consistency. Prioritize on chest and legs.

💥

Crit Damage ~150%

Once crit rate is capped, stack crit damage. Primary damage multiplier for all builds.

⚡

Substat Priority

Additional Power > ATK Power Up > DEF Ignore. Reforge gear to roll these in order.

🔥 Element-Specific Build Logic

Venom / Poison (Johnny + Charlotte)

  • Johnny — Primary Venom DPS, maintains Poison on field. 2-piece Venomfog Shade amplifies Poison damage.
  • Charlotte — Needs Johnny to maintain Poison to reach burst ceiling. The two are designed for each other.

Blaze / Burn (Aria + Kalien + Kalsion)

  • Aria — Enabler. Applies both Burn AND Airborne simultaneously. Best setup hero for Reina and Kalsion. Uses 3-piece Blazing Flame.
  • Kalien — Rapidly stacks Burn through fox clones. Fox Grab and Finale deliver burst + Stun.
  • Kalsion — Heavy hitter. Weapon Blaze enhances damage, but needs Airborne and Burn enablers to peak.

Frost / Freeze (Othello + Cerese + Dana)

  • Cerese — Best Frost applier. Multi-hit damage + strong control grouping. Weakness: large bosses (long animations, Ice Storm disadvantage).
  • Othello — Frostbite DPS. Needs fast Frost stacking to trigger max damage window. Exclusive Karma compensates for low base crit passive.
  • Dana — Flexible. Deals Frost off-field, uses familiar to牵制 enemies, provides team shield after perfect parry.

Bleed / Stun (Roxy + Theresia + Astria)

  • Roxy — Fast Bleed stacking at all ranges, but needs Stun to maximize damage. Theresia and Astria are natural partners.
  • Theresia — On-field DPS. Applies both Bleed and heavy Stun simultaneously.
  • Astria — Trades mobility for damage. Rain-like arrows stack Stun and Break. Outputs while teammates control enemies. Crit rate is highest priority.

Gale / Airborne (Reina + Eileen)

  • Reina — Single-target specialist. Long skill combo chains on airborne enemies. 2-piece Azure Gale directly amplifies Gale damage. Aria is best enabler.
  • Eileen — Supports Airborne from offensive angle. Boosts team move speed and ATK. Works well as a fast switch character with solid crit. Most flexible gear (Ballad of Life or Ruinsbreaker).

Thunder / Shock (Ornette)

  • Ornette — Powerful Thunderbolt hero that can lead most teams. Peaks in Shock teams. 2-piece Thunderrush is mandatory.
  • When Sion and Tarte aren't available, Lute works as substitute partner (healing and ATK buff sustain Ornette's output).

🌟 Special Heroes

⚔️

Lute

One of only two healers in the game (other is Tarte). Universal pick for almost any team. First awakening node boosts team ATK. Fits into Knockout and Stun teams. 2-piece Holy Arbiter provides healing output, Ruinsbreaker maintains personal damage.

✨

Tarte

The other healer. Build is still in final testing. Value rises significantly in high-difficulty content where survival becomes the bottleneck.

🗡️ Exclusive Karma Acquisition

Exclusive Karma weapons (like Johnny's Wandering Blade or Cerese's Eternal Blizzard) require Fragments of Afterimage and Memory to obtain.

  • Farming these materials is the main progression gate between functional builds and fully optimized builds
  • Prioritize farming fragments for heroes you plan to main before chasing perfect rune substats
  • Basic Karma can be purchased in the Exchange shop and is not hero-specific
  • Use 7 identical basic Karma to ascend to max level (1 base + 6 replicas)
  • World bosses can drop basic Karma (RNG), farmable daily

🤝 Team Building Principle

💡 Key Insight

Most DPS heroes need at least one specific condition to reach their damage ceiling:

  • Reina and Kalsion need Airborne
  • Othello needs Frost stacks
  • Charlotte needs Poison on field
  • Roxy needs Stun

Before finalizing a team, identify what your main DPS needs and ensure at least one teammate can reliably apply it.
