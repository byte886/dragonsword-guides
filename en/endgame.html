Endgame Guide

After the story, the real game begins: Tower of Trials, Lv90 Hunts and Raids, Exclusive Karma ascension, perfect gear reforging and 100% collection. Build a daily/weekly routine to steadily progress toward min-maxed heroes.

📖 Reading time: ~7 min 🏆 7 Endgame Activities ⏱️ 100+ Hours
⚡ Quick Answer

Endgame = Tower of Trials (3 towers, unique Karma rewards) + Lv90 Hunts/Raids + min-maxing + 100% collection. Daily: 3 World Bosses (105 medals) + dailies + currency dungeons. Weekly: high-tier Raids + Hunts + Tower progression. Spend Exchange Medals on Emblems (rarest resource, needed for Lv60-70 breakthrough). Total playtime: 150-200 hours for full completion.

🏆 7 Core Endgame Activities

1. Tower of Trials (试炼之塔)

  • Location: Skyspire Rock mountain
  • The current premier endgame challenge
  • Clearing unlocks unique Karma (weapon cards)
  • Primary late-game source of Fragments of Memory
  • Source of Whirling Thoughts (for Exclusive Karma crafting)
  • There are 3 towers to conquer

2. High-Difficulty Raids (鏖战副本)

  • Unlock at Lv50, max difficulty Lv90
  • Drop Runes and high-tier materials
  • Lv62+ guarantees SSR runes per clear
  • Long-term best-in-slot source for free players
  • Up to 3-player co-op

3. Hunts (讨伐/狩猎)

  • World bosses and elite monsters
  • Max difficulty Lv90
  • Drop Record of Battle (skill upgrade material)
  • Drop Karma cards and duplicates
  • Daily refresh — farm every day

4. World Bosses (世界Boss)

  • Scattered across open world areas
  • Daily respawn
  • Most reliable source of Basic Karma duplicates
  • Drop Hourglasses and Exchange Medals
  • 3 easy World Bosses = 105 Exchange Medals/day
  • Pick bosses matching your main DPS element

5. Min-Maxing (极致数值优化)

  • All 19 heroes at max level, max breakthrough
  • All Exclusive Karma obtained and ascended to Rank VI
  • Gear at 5-star +15 with perfect reforged substats
  • Optimal rune configurations
  • All skills maxed

6. Collection & Completion (收集与完成度)

  • Adventure Manual: 149 entries, 5 chapters
  • Achievements: 103 total, 5 categories
  • Title system
  • 100% map exploration
  • Familiar collection: 29 total
  • Cooking recipes: 73 standard dishes

7. Remaining Side Quests

  • Unfinished side content after story completion
  • Hero Quests (character-specific missions)
  • Regional tasks

📅 Daily & Weekly Routine

Daily Tasks

  1. Defeat 3 easy World Bosses → 105 Exchange Medals
  2. Complete daily commissions
  3. Run currency dungeons (gold, ores, XP books)
  4. Check Sudden Missions for Fragments of Sacred Light (Awakening material)

Weekly Tasks

  1. High-difficulty Raids (shared weekly reward cap)
  2. High-difficulty Hunts
  3. Progress in Tower of Trials
  4. Check weekly-reset events

Long-Term Goals

  1. All heroes max level + max breakthrough
  2. Perfect gear and runes for main team
  3. All Exclusive Karma obtained
  4. 100% achievements
  5. 100% map exploration
  6. 100% Adventure Manual

⚠️ Critical Resource Tip

Emblems are the rarest endgame resource. They only drop randomly from rare chests with no reliable farm method (except Exchange). Heroes need large amounts for Lv60-70 breakthrough. Spend your Exchange Medals on Emblems first — they're the #1 progression bottleneck.

💡 Endgame Pro Tips

1

Medals → Emblems

Always spend Exchange Medals on Emblems. They're the rarest resource and the biggest progression bottleneck for Lv60-70 heroes.

2

3 World Bosses Daily

Three easy World Bosses give 105 medals/day. This is your most reliable daily income — never skip it.

3

Focus 1-2 DPS

Don't spread resources thin. Pick 1-2 main DPS heroes and max them before investing in others.

4

Tower First

Prioritize Tower of Trials progression. It's the source of Fragments of Memory and Whirling Thoughts for Exclusive Karma.

5

Don't Burn Out

Endgame is a marathon, not a sprint. Pace your weekly routine — 150-200 hours is a lot of content.

6

Check Roadmap

The game is newly released. New content, heroes and features are coming. Check the roadmap regularly.
