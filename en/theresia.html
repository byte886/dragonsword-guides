Theresia — The Inquisitor

One of only ten Heretic Inquisitors with execution authority. The most self-sufficient Bleed and Stun DPS in the game — applies both ailments alone, no setup partner required.

Twin Maces
Amplitude Trait

⚡ Quick Answer

Theresia is the best aggressive carry in the game. She applies both Bleed AND Stun by herself, making her the easiest hero to build a team around. Core loop: Consecration (Bleed) → Celestial Verdict (Stun) → switch to Roxy for burst. Best team: Theresia + Roxy + Lute.

⚔️ All Skills Explained

Skill Type Effect
Consecration Active Heavy forward strike that leaves a spinning mace. When activated, extends the basic attack chain. Applies Bleed.
Celestial Verdict Active Jump and slam the ground, creating a shockwave on impact. Applies Stun. Opens Signal Skill windows for teammates.
Divine Arc Signal Skill Triggers on Stunned enemies. Dashes to target and applies Bleed. Main burst skill — one of the highest damage skills in the game.
Holy Rise Signal Skill Triggers on Bleeding enemies. Re-applies Bleed and launches target Airborne. Integrates Theresia into Airborne teams.

🔄 Core Combo Loop

The Theresia One-Hero Setup

Unlike most heroes, Theresia doesn't need a partner to set up her ailments. She does it all herself:

  1. Consecration — Apply Bleed, extend attack chain
  2. Celestial Verdict — Slam for Stun, opens Signal window
  3. Switch to teammate — Roxy or another hero triggers their Signal Skill on the Stunned enemy
  4. Switch back — Continue pressure with Divine Arc on Stunned targets
💡 Key Insight

Because Theresia applies both Bleed and Stun alone, she's the most flexible team anchor in the game. Any hero that responds to either ailment works with her.

Why This Build

  • 3-pc Ruinsbreaker — DEF Ignore on Signal Skills (10s, 20% DEF penetration). Critical for boss damage.
  • 2-pc Holy Arbiter — +15% damage to Stunned enemies. Perfectly matches Theresia's Stun-focused playstyle.
  • Resolve — Reliable damage boost. Swap to Amplification x5 if you want to gamble on max crit damage.

👹 Boss Fight Loop

  1. Open with Theresia's Celestial Verdict to apply Stun
  2. Once enemy is Stunned, switch to Roxy to trigger her Signal Skill
  3. Continue output through Roxy while target is controlled
  4. Use third hero (Lute for healing, Castella for extra Stun) to complete the loop
  5. Switch back to Theresia and repeat — Consecration → Celestial Verdict → Divine Arc burst

🎮 Combat Tips

1

Self-Sufficient Setup

Theresia doesn't need teammates to set up ailments. She applies Bleed + Stun alone — the only hero who can do both reliably.

2

Consecration First

Always open with Consecration to apply Bleed and extend your attack chain before using Celestial Verdict for Stun.

3

Divine Arc = Burst

Divine Arc on Stunned enemies is your main damage source. One of the highest burst skills in the entire game.

4

Holy Rise for Airborne

Holy Rise launches Bleeding enemies Airborne. Use this to integrate Theresia into Airborne team compositions.

5

Wide Mace Range

Twin maces have wider reach than most melee weapons. You'll rarely miss against groups, making her great for trash clears.

6

Awaken First

At 6★ awakening, passives and enhanced skills unlock. Only then is the crit stat line worth investing in.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Wrong element Karma — Always use her Exclusive Karma (Merciful Death Sentence), not a generic weapon
  • 5-piece same set — Never use 5 of one set. 3+2 mixing (Ruinsbreaker + Holy Arbiter) is always better
  • Ignoring DEF Ignore — DEF Ignore from Ruinsbreaker is critical for boss damage. Don't skip it
  • Pure DPS mindset — Theresia's power comes from ailments, not raw attacks. Always be applying Bleed/Stun
  • Early Exclusive Karma investment — Wait until you're sure Theresia is your main DPS before farming her exclusive materials
