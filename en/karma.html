Progression System

Karma System Guide

Karma are ability cards slotted into a hero's weapon. Each card carries base stats + special traits, and the strongest Exclusive Karma can rewrite how skills behave in combat. The biggest power spike comes from ascension, not leveling.

📖 Reading time: ~8 min 🃏 16 Exclusive Karma ⬆️ Rank VI Max
⚡ Quick Answer

Karma is a weapon-slot ability card separate from gear and hero level. Two types: Basic (any matching element hero) and Exclusive (one specific hero only, best-in-slot). Power comes from ascension (feed duplicate copies, max Rank VI, needs 5 copies total). Always match element first — mismatched Karma traits don't work. Farm World Bosses daily for Basic Karma duplicates; use Shanoa to craft Fragment of Afterimage for Exclusive Karma.

🃏 Two Types of Karma

Type Usable By Main Effect
Basic Karma Any hero with matching element Stats and conditional bonuses. Reusable across multiple builds.
Exclusive Karma One specific hero only Strong stats + hero-specific passive that modifies skills. Best-in-slot for dedicated DPS.
📜

Basic Karma Growth

Grows through leveling, breakthrough and duplicate ascension. Obtained via regular Karma cards and currency paths.

⭐

Exclusive Karma Growth

Requires Exclusive Karma resources for long-term growth. Obtained via Hero Logs, Tower of Trials exchange, dismantle value and conversion.

⬆️ Ascension System (The Real Power Spike)

The real power increase comes from ascension, not the level bar. Here's how it works:

  • Ascension only accepts exact duplicate copies of the same Karma
  • Base cards only show starting stats. Feeding duplicates unlocks and enhances special traits
  • Maximum rank: Rank VI — requires 5 duplicate copies total
  • Each rank enhances traits (extra crit, more ailment damage, skill-specific modifiers)
  • Always open the preview before submitting a duplicate to see what the next copy unlocks
⚠️ Pro Tip

Don't waste duplicates on heroes you don't main. Ascension is permanent and resource-intensive. Pick 1-2 DPS heroes and focus all Karma investment on them.

🔥 Element Matching Requirement

Technically you can slot any Karma on any hero, but Karma cards carry an element that must match the hero's element.

  • If elements don't match, the trait line won't activate properly
  • Example: Frost hero Reina needs Frost Karma. A Bleed or Physical card wastes important effects
  • Match element first, then worry about ascension

🎯 How to Get Karma Cards

Basic Karma

  • Campaign progress gives a few cards
  • Others obtained through purchase or farming
  • Purchase currency comes from random treasure chests
  • Most reliable: Tower of Trials (after unlocking)
  • World Bosses are the most reliable daily source for Basic Karma duplicates — pick the boss matching your main DPS element

Exclusive Karma

  • Hero Quests (complete to obtain)
  • Tower of Trials exchange
  • Dismantle unwanted Exclusive Karma for resources
  • Craft via Fragment of Afterimage (see below)

✨ Fragment of Afterimage (Exclusive Karma Crafting)

Fragment of Afterimage is the currency used to exchange for Exclusive Karma. Each Exclusive Karma card costs 1 fragment.

How to Craft

The only way to obtain Fragment of Afterimage is through conversion at Alchemist Shanoa:

Shanoa Conversion Recipe

Material Amount
Whirling Thoughts1
Fragments of Memory100
Gold100,000
Result1 Fragment of Afterimage

Shanoa's location: Near the cathedral in Orbis Royal Castle.

Fragment of Memory Uses

  • Upgrade Basic Karma
  • Convert to Fragment of Afterimage at Shanoa
  • Sources: Treasure chests, quests, first-time Trial clears, mercenary level ups, Tower of Trials
  • Late-game main source: Tower of Trials (located at Skyspire Rock mountain)

📜 Karma System Core Principles

1

Match Element First

Karma must match hero element or traits won't activate. This is non-negotiable.

2

Ascend Basic Karma First

Equip matching-element Basic Karma on your main DPS, then feed duplicates to Rank VI.

3

Exclusive Is Long-Term

Only invest in Exclusive Karma for 1-2 heroes you actually play. It's a massive resource sink.

4

Farm World Bosses Daily

Most reliable source for Basic Karma duplicates. Do this every day.

5

Tower of Trials = Material Core

Late-game primary source for Fragments of Memory. Prioritize unlocking and farming it.

6

Dismantle Unwanted Exclusive

Got Exclusive Karma for a hero you don't use? Dismantle it for resources to craft ones you need.

7

Preview Before Ascending

Always check the preview to see what the next duplicate unlocks before committing.
