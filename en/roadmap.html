Post-Launch Plan

Update Roadmap 2026

Hound13 has committed to long-term support with 4 free new heroes, a Rift System, Hero Requests, Sky Island expansion and PS5/Switch 2 ports. Here's everything confirmed, under consideration, and not planned.

🗓️ Updated: Aug 2026 🦸 4 Free Heroes 🌍 Sky Island Expansion
⚡ Quick Summary

4 free new heroes coming in H2 2026 (no paid characters ever). Rift System adds intense instanced combat. Hero Requests expand character stories (Othello first, then Liza). Sky Island is World 2 expansion (may be paid DLC). PS5 and Switch 2 ports confirmed. First week sales: 200,000+ copies. No gacha, no Battle Pass, no P2W — ever.

✅ Confirmed Content

1. Four Free New Heroes (H2 2026)

  • All 4 new heroes are free — not paid DLC
  • Each has unique story, gear and mechanics
  • Rolling out over the coming weeks and months
  • Base game: 19 heroes → Final total: 23 heroes
  • Dana has been revealed as one of the upcoming heroes

2. Hero Requests (Character Story Missions)

  • Additional quests exploring hero stories not covered in the main campaign
  • Starts with Othello's story, followed by Liza's story
  • Runs parallel to the main storyline

3. Rift System (裂缝系统)

  • Rifts appear in the world
  • Enter through rifts to battle in other dimensions
  • Designed for more intense combat encounters
  • New endgame instanced content

4. Sky Island / Floating Island (天空岛扩展)

  • New region — World 2
  • Adventure on floating islands in the sky
  • Distribution model TBD — may be free or paid DLC
  • Releases after Orbis (World 1) story DLC is completed

5. Platform Ports

  • PS5 version confirmed
  • Nintendo Switch 2 version confirmed
  • Currently PC-only (Steam)
  • No release dates announced for console versions

6. Swimsuit DLC (Cosmetic Only)

  • Swimsuit costumes for 3 female characters (Reina, Cerese, and one more)
  • Purely cosmetic — does not affect gameplay balance

7. Quality Improvements

⚔️ Combat

Better hit impact, smoother controls

📷 Camera

Camera upgrades and fixes

🎮 Controller

Improved controller support

🖥️ UI / UX

Interface additions, accessibility improvements

🌐 Localization

Translation and voice quality enhancements

✨ Polish

General bug fixes and quality of life

❌ No Current Plans

These features have been explicitly ruled out (for now):

Feature Notes
Housing No player housing / home system planned
Fishing No fishing minigame planned
Battle Pass No battle pass / seasonal pass system
Pause Feature No pause function (likely due to action combat design)
Gacha System Never — the game explicitly abandoned gacha monetization
Paid Characters All heroes are free — no paid DLC characters
P2W Mechanics No pay-to-win elements of any kind

💰 Monetization Model

Confirmed Paid Content

  1. Base game: $29.99 one-time purchase
  2. Cosmetic DLC: Swimsuits and other appearance items (balance-neutral)
  3. Familiar DLC: Exploration companions (e.g., Griffon)
  4. Sky Island expansion: Potentially paid (TBD)

Explicitly NOT Doing

❌ Gacha

No loot boxes, no character pulls

❌ Paid Heroes

All characters free forever

❌ Battle Pass

No seasonal pass system

❌ P2W

No pay-to-win mechanics

💡 Long-Term Support

Hound13 has committed to long-term support for DragonSword: Awakening. Even after transitioning from live-service to a premium game model, the studio continues to invest in content updates and quality improvements. The strong first-week sales (200,000+ copies) provide a solid foundation for ongoing development.

🎯 What This Means for Players

Positives

  • Free heroes: 4 new characters at no extra cost = great value
  • Content expansion: Rift System and Sky Island add meaningful endgame content
  • Quality improvements: Combat, camera, UI getting ongoing polish
  • Multi-platform: PS5 and Switch 2 versions bring the game to more players
  • No-gacha promise: Sticking to the one-time purchase model, no P2W

Things to Watch

  • Update pace: New content rolls out gradually, not all at once
  • Sky Island pricing: World 2 expansion may be paid DLC
  • Unconfirmed features: Photo Mode, open-world co-op still under consideration
  • Missing features: Housing, fishing, Battle Pass will not be added
