Understanding the Character System

DragonSword: Awakening uses a unique tag-team combat system built around three heroes rather than a single character. Here's what you need to know:

Combat Flow

  1. Apply Status Ailments — Each hero applies their primary status effect through attacks
  2. Trigger Signal Skills — When compatible ailments stack on an enemy, powerful Signal Skills activate
  3. Switch Heroes — Tag out at the right moment to trigger Switching Signals
  4. Chain Combos — Continue the combo chain for massive damage, control, or Break pressure

Team Building Principle

💡 Team Compatibility > Individual Damage

A well-synergized team of B-tier heroes will outperform a group of S-tier heroes with poor ailment synergy. Focus on covering multiple status types and creating combo chains.

How to Unlock Heroes

  • Story / Progress — 8 heroes unlock naturally through the main story and Hero Quests
  • Exchange Shop — 11 heroes cost 1 Fate's Invitation each, obtainable through gameplay
  • No Gacha — There is no random character draw system. Every hero is earnable
