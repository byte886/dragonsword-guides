Cooking & Recipes Guide

Cooking transforms raw materials gathered across Orbis into healing foods, party buffs and even ally revival. 73 standard dishes across three preparation methods. First cook permanently registers the recipe.

📖 Reading time: ~8 min 🍳 73 Recipes 🔥 3 Cooking Methods
⚡ Quick Answer

Cook every dish once to permanently register it. Three methods: Grilling (healing), Boiling (buffs + revival), Slicing (stamina management). Always carry at least one Revival Tea before difficult fights. 5-star results need 4-star ingredients. Wrong combinations produce poisonous Mysterious Dish.

🔥 Three Cooking Methods

🍖

Grilling (29 dishes)

Recovery dishes for HP and stamina. Your everyday healing food. Best for open world exploration and casual combat.

🍲

Boiling (26 dishes)

Buff stews and revival teas. 15-minute party buffs and ally resurrection. Essential before boss fights.

🥗

Slicing (18 dishes)

Stamina and status management dishes. Reduce Familiar flight/swim/climb stamina consumption. Great for exploration.

📜 Recipe Registration & Star System

  • You start with no complete recipe book — each dish must be cooked once to be registered permanently
  • Ingredient quality determines result strength. Same-effect dishes can vary in power based on ingredient tier
  • 5-star results: Multi-ingredient recipes use 3 four-star ingredients. Single-ingredient recipes (like Grilled Meat) use 3x base ingredient
  • Wrong combinations produce Mysterious Dish — it's poisonous, meaning the recipe is incorrect

👨‍🍳 How to Cook

  1. Interact with a cooking station (stove)
  2. Go to the Ingredients tab
  3. Select the correct preparation method (Grilling / Boiling / Slicing)
  4. Add the correct ingredients
  5. Start cooking

📜 Cooking Core Principles

1

Register First

Every dish must be cooked once to be permanently saved in your recipe book. Cook everything as you gather ingredients.

2

Stars Depend on Ingredients

5-star needs 4-star ingredients. Low-tier ingredients still register the recipe, just with weaker effects.

3

Method = Purpose

Grilling for daily healing, Boiling for boss prep (buffs + revival), Slicing for exploration stamina.

4

Wrong = Poison

Mysterious Dish means your combination is wrong. Check the recipe and try again.

5

Revival Tea Essential

Always carry at least one Revival Tea before difficult fights. It's your only revive option.

6

Buffs Last 15 Minutes

Eat stews right before boss fights. 15 minutes is enough for most encounters.
