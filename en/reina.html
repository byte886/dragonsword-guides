Orbis Kingdom · S Tier

Reina — The Wind That Protects

The primary Airborne launcher in the game and one of the strongest juggle characters. Keeps enemies helpless in the air, limiting their counter-attack opportunities. High mobility with sprint + separate dodge.

Gale Sword
Gale Trait
Airborne / Bleed
8th Order Commander
⚡ Quick Answer

Reina is the best Airborne launcher and the core of every juggle team. Launch enemies with Rising Cutter or Wind Slayer, extend with Wind Grab, and finish with Castella's ground slam. Best team: Reina + Castella + Lute. Build with 3 Ruinsbreaker + 2 Azure Gale. Airborne is one of the strongest mechanics because launched enemies can't counter-attack.

🪽 Airborne Combo Sequence

Reina + Castella + Lute — Full Juggle Loop

  1. Open with Reina — Use Rising Cutter or Wind Slayer to launch the target
  2. Tempest immediately — As the enemy starts rising, execute Reina's Tempest Signal Skill
  3. Switch to Lute — Activate Lute's Signal Skill to extend Airborne duration
  4. Cut to Castella — Slam the target to the ground for massive damage
  5. Repeat — Loop to maintain Airborne window control
💡 Why Airborne Is Broken

Airborne is one of the strongest mechanics in the game because enemies suspended in the air have limited counter-attack opportunities. They can't block, can't trigger Super Armor, and can't use special attacks. A well-executed juggle = a free damage window.

💨 Mobility: The "Mobile Bridge"

Reina has unique mobility options that make her the "bridge" of any team:

  • Sprint-style movement — Long dash to recover space
  • Separate dodge — Short evasion for committed attacks
  • Two reposition options — Use the long sprint to recover spacing, save the short dodge for the next committed attack

✅ Strengths

🪽

S-Tier Airborne Core

The primary Airborne launcher. Essential for every juggle team composition.

⚡

Dual Launchers

Both Rising Cutter and Wind Slayer apply Airborne. Flexible combo openers for any situation.

🔄

Dual Signal Skills

Tempest (Stun trigger) and Wind Grab (Airborne trigger). Responds to two different ailments.

💨

High Mobility

Sprint + separate dodge = two reposition options. Best mobility kit in the game.

🎪

Best Juggler

Keeps enemies in the air, limiting their counter-attack. Creates longer attack chains in tough encounters.

🔧

Universal Fit

Can slot into many teams as the third member. Works with Fire, Ice, Bleed and Poison archetypes.

❌ Weaknesses

  • Airborne dependent — Against Super Armor enemies, Airborne fails and Reina loses significant value
  • Needs teammates — Best performance requires finishers like Castella to capitalize on Airborne windows
  • Melee risk — Despite high mobility, she's still melee and can be punished
  • Bleed is secondary — Tempest's Bleed is a bonus effect, not her primary damage source
  • Execution required — Needs precise Airborne timing and switch rhythm to maximize juggle duration

🎮 Combat Tips

  1. Open with launchers — Always start with Rising Cutter or Wind Slayer to get enemies airborne
  2. Tempest on Stun — When teammates apply Stun, switch to Reina and trigger Tempest for Bleed
  3. Wind Grab to extend — Use Wind Grab immediately after launching to keep enemies in the air longer
  4. Use both dodges — Long sprint recovers space, short dodge for attack gaps. Don't waste either
  5. Pair with Castella — Before Airborne window ends, switch to Castella for the ground-slam finisher
  6. Lute extends chains — In Airborne teams, Lute can further extend Airborne duration
  7. Keep them airborne — Enemies in Airborne state have limited counter-attack. Juggle = safety.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌

Forcing Airborne on Super Armor

Against Super Armor enemies, Airborne is ineffective. Switch to a different strategy or bring armor breakers.

❌

Not Switching After Launch

Launching and then staying on Reina wastes the Airborne damage window. Switch to finishers immediately.

❌

Ignoring Tempest Bleed

Tempest's Bleed is a free extra damage source. Always use it when Stun is applied.

❌

Wasting Dual Dodges

Reina's mobility is her biggest advantage. Use both dodge options strategically.

❌

Playing Like Ranged

Reina is a melee Airborne character. If you want ranged, pick Cerese or Othello.

❌

No Azure Gale Set

2-pc Azure Gale is critical for Airborne damage. Don't skip it.
