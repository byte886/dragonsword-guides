Eileen — Offensive Support

A chivalric knight who bridges Gale and Airborne combos. Eileen is an offensive support who enables Reina's Airborne juggles while adding respectable damage and survivability.

Orbis Kingdom
Rapier / Wind Magic
Airborne
Offensive Support / Airborne Enabler
⚡ Quick Answer

Eileen is an A-tier offensive support. Equip Karma "Between Chivalry and Bookmarks", 3-piece Azure Gale + 2-piece Ballad of Life (or Ruinsbreaker for more damage), and prioritize ATK, Crit DMG, and Max HP. Her best team is Reina + Kalsion — she enables Reina's Airborne combos while Kalsion provides Burn pressure. She also pairs well with Aria and Cerese.

🎯 Playstyle

Eileen is a Gale-element support who excels at enabling Airborne combos. Her skills launch enemies and keep them suspended, allowing Reina to extend juggles. Unlike pure supports, she builds with offensive stats — Azure Gale boosts her Gale damage while Ballad of Life provides sustain. She is the bridge between setup and burst in Airborne teams.

💡 Combat Tips

Launch First

Use Wind Launcher to start Airborne combos, then swap to Reina for the juggle.

Balance Offense and Survival

Azure Gale + Ballad of Life gives her damage and sustain — don't go pure glass cannon.

Max HP Matters

Her build includes Max HP because she needs to survive while setting up combos.

Extend Juggles

Gale Cyclone keeps enemies suspended longer, giving Reina more time to burst.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Building Eileen as pure DPS — she is an enabler first, damage dealer second
  • Using Gale Cyclone before enemies are Airborne — it needs the launch setup
  • Ignoring Max HP stats — her survivability is part of her value
