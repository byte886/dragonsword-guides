Alex — Tank

A reliable frontline tank with high HP and damage reduction. Alex absorbs hits and creates space for DPS teammates, with Knockdown utility that sets up juggle combos.

⚡ Quick Answer

Alex is a B-tier tank who excels at absorbing damage and breaking enemy poise with Knockdown. His optimal build is still being tested by the community, but prioritize HP and survivability. His best team is Ornette + Castella for stagger control against armored bosses. He is also a solid early-game frontliner paired with Aria and Roxy.

📝 Note

Alex's optimal Karma, gear, and rune builds are still being tested by the community as of August 2026. Recommendations on this page are provisional and will be updated as the meta settles.

🎯 Playstyle

Alex is the team's shield. He has high health, damage reduction, and poise-breaking attacks that Knockdown enemies. While his individual damage is low, his ability to survive boss attacks and create openings makes him valuable in tough fights. He breaks enemy poise, then teammates like Castella capitalize on Knockdown targets with launchers.

💡 Combat Tips

Prioritize Divine Wind

Level this core skill first — it is Alex's main poise break and Knockdown tool.

Guard at Key Moments

Use guard during boss charge attacks to reduce damage and create counter windows.

Knockdown Setup

After breaking poise, Knockdown the enemy then swap to Castella for Airborne follow-up.

Don't Chase Damage

Alex's value is survival and control, not DPS. Build him tanky.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Building Alex as a DPS — he is a tank/control character
  • Forgetting to guard — his damage reduction is a major asset
  • Leaving Alex on-field too long — swap to DPS after Knockdown is applied
