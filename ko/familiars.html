Familiars Guide

Familiars are traversal mounts that unlock shortly after joining Johnny's Mercenaries. There are 29 Familiars across 8 types, each granting a unique movement skill like Air Dash, Underwater Dash, or Boost Jump.

Updated: 2026-08-25 29 Familiars 8 Types

What Are Familiars?

Familiars are ridable companions that help you traverse the open world of Orbis. They unlock early in Chapter 1 after joining Johnny's Mercenaries. Each Familiar belongs to one of eight types, and each type grants a specific traversal skill.

Key facts:

  • 29 Familiars total — 18 free through gameplay, 10 DLC-exclusive, 1 Deluxe Pack-exclusive
  • No combat stats — Familiars are purely for traversal and type passives
  • Most are claimed from the Familiar Codex after meeting a requirement
  • The Abyssal Direwolf was a free pre-order bonus until August 31, 2026

Type Passive System

When you own 2 or more Familiars of the same type, you unlock that type's passive: keeping one Familiar of that type in your party grants its movement skill to all Familiars in the party.

For example, owning 2 Direwolves means any equipped Familiar can use Boost Jump. This is why collecting duplicates within a type is valuable — you can use your favorite mount while having access to multiple movement skills.

Type passive bonuses:

  1. The type's traversal skill becomes available
  2. All Familiars can use that skill
  3. Stamina consumption reduced by 20% during that movement type
  4. Movement speed increased by 10% during that movement type

Note: The Brave Baby Dragon (Dragon type) does not provide a type passive.

Which to Unlock First

For the biggest early exploration upgrades, prioritize these Familiars:

1. Bright Crowtit (Bird)

Grants Air Dash — the single biggest exploration upgrade. Reach Novice Mercenary Corps rank.

2. Refreshing Canary (Bird)

Second Bird for the type passive — all mounts get Air Dash. Complete 20 Mini-Games.

3. Marple Flying Squirrel (Squirrel)

Climbing Jump for cliffs and high places. Reach Intermediate Mercenary Corps.

4. Stormy Direwolf (Direwolf)

Boost Jump for crossing wide gaps. Reach Veteran Mercenary Corps.

5. Mist Wing Cat (Cat)

Swim Dash for water exploration. Craft 100 equipment pieces.

Tips & Common Mistakes

Claim from the Codex

Meeting a requirement doesn't auto-unlock the Familiar — you must claim it in the Familiar Codex.

Get 2 of Each Type

Two Familiars of a type share the skill across all your mounts. This is a major mobility upgrade.

DLC Is Not Stronger

DLC Familiars provide the same skills as free ones. They only add variety and help hit type passive thresholds.

No Combat Function

Familiars have no combat stats. You must dismount to fight — monster attacks knock you off.
